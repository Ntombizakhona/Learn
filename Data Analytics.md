# Learn Data Analytics

Diving into data science can feel overwhelming, but the good news is there’s a wealth of resources out there to guide you—especially if you’re just getting started or looking to refresh your foundational knowledge.

Here’s a mix of theory, hands-on practice, and expert insights to help you on your way.

## 1. Learn By Putting Theory Into Practice
### **CognitiveClass.ai**
CognitiveClass.ai, formerly known as Big Data University, offers a comprehensive learning path titled *Applied Data Science with Python*.

This series of courses is beginner-friendly and provides a blend of theoretical knowledge and practical skills.

You’ll learn how to use Python for data cleaning, analysis, and visualization, gaining hands-on experience through guided lectures and labs.

This learning path is ideal for those looking to solidify their Python skills before diving into more advanced topics like machine learning and big data.

*If I’m being honest, this might be the only course you need.*

---

## 2. Learn By Reading A Book
### **Data Analytics & Visualization All-in-One For Dummies**
Books offer the opportunity to absorb material at your own pace.

Data Analytics & Visualization All-in-One For Dummies is a comprehensive resource that compiles essential information on mining, organizing, and communicating data.

Spanning approximately 850 pages, this reference delivers eight books in one, helping you build a solid foundation in data wrangling and analytics.

It’s an excellent starting point for those who prefer structured, in-depth reading.

*I know you’re not a dummy, but if you’re someone like me who likes absorbing knowledge that is quietly packaged in a book, this might be the one.*

---

## 3. Learn By Building A Project
### **Build Real Projects with Kaggle**
Practical experience is crucial in data science.

Kaggle provides access to a plethora of real-world datasets that you can use to hone your skills.

> Beyond datasets, Kaggle hosts competitions where you can submit your models and potentially win monetary prizes.
> Additionally, Kaggle offers courses to further your learning.
> Engaging with Kaggle allows you to build a portfolio while applying your knowledge in real scenarios. 
>
> You learn data science by doing, and once you get your hands on it, I hear it can be quite a thrilling field.

---

## 4. Learn By Watching A Video
### **Udemy’s Data Science Courses**
For visual learners, video courses can be incredibly effective.

Udemy offers a variety of data science courses that cover topics ranging from Python programming to machine learning.
These courses often include bite-sized lessons, making complex concepts more digestible.

One notable course is the Data+, which provides a comprehensive overview of data science tools and techniques.   

*I love going through Comptia courses on Udemy, they’re really a great refresher or a great introduction.*

---
## 5. Learn By Listening
### Secrets of Data Analytics Leaders
If you prefer learning on the go, podcasts are a great option.

Secrets of Data Analytics Leaders, hosted by Wayne Eckerson, features interviews with data and analytics professionals from Fortune 2000 organizations.

The podcast delves into the latest technologies, techniques, and trends in the fast-paced world of data science, offering valuable insights from industry leaders.  

This podcast is really good, I feel like I am in the room with these people, instead of music, I say listen to this for a few months.

---

## 6. Learn By Pursuing A Certification
### AWS Certified Data Engineer – Associate
Pursuing a certification can validate your skills and provide a structured learning path.

The AWS Certified Data Engineer – Associate certification is designed for individuals who want to demonstrate their expertise in data-related AWS services, data pipeline implementation, and performance optimization.

The exam consists of 65 questions and has a duration of 130 minutes. Earning this certification can enhance your credibility in the job market.

It’s no secret that I’m an AWS enthusiast, their certifications really force you to delve deeper in a particularly domain, and that’s why they’re so incredibly credible and worth it!

---

## 7. Learn By Following a Blog
### Stay Current with Towards Data Science
The field of data science is continually evolving, and staying updated is essential.

Towards Data Science is a popular blog that offers a wide range of articles on topics like machine learning, data visualization, and artificial intelligence.

The platform provides insights from both industry professionals and academics, making it a valuable resource for continuous learning.

Towards Data Science recently moved from Medium to their own independent site, and they’re a good blog to follow!

---

## Concluding Remarks
Embarking on a data science journey is an exciting endeavor. By leveraging these resources—ranging from theoretical foundations to practical applications—you can build a robust skill set.

Remember, consistency and curiosity are key. Happy learning!
